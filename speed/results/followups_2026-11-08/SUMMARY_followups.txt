1. PR #4634 test fix (pr4634_test_fix.diff, task1_test_fix.json)
- Change in test_smolvla_tuple_kv_parity:
  dynamic_kv.crop(prefix_len)
  ->
  for layer in dynamic_kv.layers:
      layer.crop(prefix_len - layer.get_seq_length())
  This is the same form main uses in denoise_step since #5220 (4598a0bb).
- Why: transformers 5.22 changed DynamicLayer.crop's argument from a target length to a number of tokens to
  remove. A positive value now raises ValueError ("Calling `crop` with a positive value ... is not supported"),
  and crop(0) does nothing. Source: cache_utils.py lines 163-180 in 5.22.1, and 139-151 in 5.5.4.
- Results:
  - Today's main + PR + patch, with the fix: 2/2 in test_smolvla_compile_parity.py, and 15/15 in
    tests/policies/smolvla.
  - The PR's own stack (torch 2.11, transformers 5.5.4), unchanged: passes at a932c505 (lerobot-pr4634) and at
    the head 2d200334. So the test fails only on current main's transformers.
  - The fix needs transformers 5.22 or newer. On 5.5.4, crop(0) empties the cache and the test fails with a
    shape mismatch. Apply it with the rebase onto main.
